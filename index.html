About me

I'm the person behind the scenes who makes customer support work: the systems, the data, and the team behind every answer a customer gets. I've spent more than a decade at Locusview, a software company serving the utility construction industry, growing with it from its fifth employee to the company it is today. These days I lead our support team and run the Zendesk setup that keeps everything moving.

What I enjoy most is finding the pattern behind a problem. A growing backlog, a question customers keep asking, a response that's too slow: there's usually a better process or a smart automation waiting to be built. Right now that means teaching an AI agent to handle routine requests so our team can focus on the hard ones.

A few things I'm proud of

  • 87%fewer open tickets after rethinking how our queue works
  • 94.9%of customers happy with the help they got in 2025
  • 90%of tickets answered on time, measured against a standard I created
  • 36 minto answer our most urgent requests, down from 2 hours

What I care about

  • Fix the system, not just the ticket. If customers keep running into the same problem, the answer is a better process, a clearer article, or an automation, not a faster workaround.
  • Measure what matters. Clear SLAs, honest dashboards, and CSAT give a team something real to improve against and give leadership a view they can trust.
  • Develop the people. I hire, coach, and support engineers so they grow into the work, and I stay hands-on as the final escalation point when things get hard.
  • Work across teams. The best support outcomes come from close partnership with Engineering, Product, DevOps, and Implementation.
