About

I think of customer support as a craft. Behind every quick, helpful answer is a set of careful decisions: where a request goes, when automation steps in, what "on time" means, and who's ready for the hard cases. Those decisions are my work.

I've spent more than 11 years at Locusview, a software company serving the utility construction industry and now part of Itron. I joined as the fifth employee. Today I lead the support team and run the Zendesk platform underneath it.

  • 94.9% Customer satisfaction (CSAT) Across 2025, the first year we measured it
  • 90%+ SLA compliance on a benchmark I built from scratch From untracked to above 90% every year since 2025

02Featured project

An AI agent for routine support requests

  • Zendesk advanced AI agents
  • Launched November 2026

The problem

Routine requests like user account changes, shipment tracking, and tablet location checks are simple one at a time. Together, they pull engineers away from the cases that need them.

What I built

  • Guided conversations that walk users through basic user-management tasks
  • Automatic FedEx tracking lookups through API calls
  • Tablet location checks through the Samsung Knox API

Results so far

It launched in November 2026, so it's too early for meaningful numbers. I'll add them here once there's enough data.

What's next

Measure how many requests it resolves on its own, and use that to decide which routine requests it takes on next.

03How I work

  • Fix the system, not the symptom.

    Repeat problems get a better process, a clearer article, or an automation, not a faster workaround.

  • Measure honestly.

    Clear standards and plain dashboards give the team a real target and everyone a picture they can trust.

  • Build people, not just processes.

    I hire and coach engineers to grow into the work, and I stay hands-on for the hardest cases.

  • Close the loop with Engineering and Product.

    Support sees problems first. I make sure they reach the people who can fix them.
