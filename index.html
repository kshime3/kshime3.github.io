Support operations, built like a craft

Good support feels effortless to the customer. Getting it there takes careful work behind the scenes: thoughtful routing, smart automation, clear standards, and a well-coached team. That's the work I love.

About

I think of customer support as a craft. Behind every quick, helpful answer is a set of decisions someone made with care: where a request goes, when an automation steps in, what "on time" really means, and who's ready for the hard cases. Those decisions are my work.

I've practiced it for more than a decade at Locusview, a software company serving the utility construction industry, where I started as the fifth employee. Today I lead the support team and the Zendesk platform underneath it. My current project: teaching an AI agent to handle routine requests, so the people on our team can spend their time on the hard ones.

How I work

  • Fix the system, not the symptom. When the same problem keeps showing up, the answer is a better process, a clearer article, or a well-placed automation, not a faster workaround.
  • Measure honestly. Clear standards and plain dashboards give a team something real to improve against, and give everyone a picture they can trust.
  • Build people, not just processes. I hire and coach engineers so they grow into the work, and I stay hands-on for the hardest cases.
  • Make it with others. The best support comes from close partnership with Engineering, Product, DevOps, and Implementation.
